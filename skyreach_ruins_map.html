The Ruins of Skyreach

Whispers Beneath the Broken Tower

For three nights, a cold blue light has burned in the shattered crown of Skyreach. The Empire’s scouts found an ancient chart there—one that marks a hidden pass through the mountains. Before they could carry it home, the horns of Chaos sounded from the eastern ridge. Now, among toppled stones and wind-bent scrub, steel meets axe beneath the watchful ruin. Whoever holds the tower’s broken walls by dusk will command the pass—and decide the fate of Oakhaven.

12 × 12 hex battlefield · North is at the top · coordinates are column letter and row number

Scenario rules

  • Turn cards: At turn start, each player draws 3 cards from their own deck. Decks contain one card for each unit: Empire Swordsmen, Archers, Knights; Chaos Orcs, Wolf Riders, Champion. Alternate one card at a time, Empire first on turn 1. Discard unplayed cards at turn end; reshuffle discards when a deck runs out.
  • Activation: A card names a unit type. When played, activate any, all, or none of your living units of that type. Each activated unit may move or attack, not both. Ranged units may attack without moving.
  • Movement: Move up to the unit's Move in adjacent hexes. A unit entering rocky ground must stop there. Terrain does not otherwise slow movement.
  • Combat: Attacker rolls its Atk dice; defender rolls its Def dice. Skulls score hits; shields cancel hits. Remove one figure per uncancelled hit. A unit on a wall hex rolls one extra defence die against ranged attacks. Tower and scrub block ranged line of sight.
  • Victory: The tower itself (F6) is impassable. Empire wins by occupying one of its surrounding wall hexes (E5, F5, E6, G6, E7, or F7) at the end of turn 10, or by destroying all Chaos units. Chaos has the same objective. If neither qualifies, the game is a draw.

Blue markers are Empire; red markers are Chaos. Every marker is one figure and one hit point. The tower blocks movement and ranged attacks through it.
