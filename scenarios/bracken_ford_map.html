The Bridge at Bracken Ford

A race for the old crossing

For three nights the river has run black beneath the pines. The Empire seized Bracken Ford to keep its supply road open; at dawn, Chaos horns answer from the eastern bank. The old bridge is the only safe crossing for horse and foot alike. Whoever holds it when the sun sinks will command the road into the heartland.

12 × 12 hex battlefield · North is at the top · Coordinates are column letter and row number

Scenario rules

  • Cards: At the start of each turn, each player draws 3 cards from their activation deck. Alternate playing one card at a time, Empire first on turn 1. Unplayed cards are discarded; shuffle discards back when the deck runs out.
  • Activation: A card names a unit type. It may activate any, all, or none of your living units of that type. Each activated unit may move or attack, not both. Ranged units may shoot instead of moving.
  • Movement: Move through adjacent hexes up to the unit's allowance. Terrain and special movement restrictions are listed below and shown on the map.
  • Combat: Click an enemy in range. The page rolls attack and defence dice automatically; each uncancelled hit removes one figure (one hit point). Ranged attacks need a clear line of sight.
  • Special terrain: Infantry cross the river at the bridge or ford; cavalry can cross only at the bridge. A unit that follows the road for at least one hex may move one extra hex, and that extra hex must also follow the road. The watchtower is impassable and blocks line of sight. Ranged units attacking from elevated ground roll +1 attack die.
  • Starting positions: Empire starts west: Swordsmen C5 and C6, Crossbowmen B4, Knights B7. Chaos starts east: Orcs H6 and H7, Goblin archers I8, Chaos warriors I5.
  • Victory: At the end of turn 10, occupy F6 or F7, or destroy every opposing unit. If both sides occupy the bridge, the side with more figures on F6–F7 wins; a tie is a draw.
