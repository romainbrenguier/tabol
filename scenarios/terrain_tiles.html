Tabletop print shop

Painted terrain for your table

Print a set of cut-out hexes for the battlefield. Realistic, painterly terrain art is cropped into pointy-top hexes and marked with map coordinates, so every road, river, ruin and hill can go in the right spot. Choose a battle, then print its sheet.

Printing tip: use A4 paper, portrait orientation, and 100% or actual size. For sturdier tiles, print on card stock, then cut around each dashed hex border. Print backgrounds must be enabled in your browser.
Terrain pieces are designed to sit on a standard pointy-top hex grid. Return to the game shelf.
