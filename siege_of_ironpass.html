The Siege of Ironpass

Fifteen units on each side · the pass must not fall

The mountain gate at Ironpass has held for a hundred winters. Now its warning fires burn out of season: a Chaos host fills the eastern valley, and a towering ogre strides before its banners. The Empire has rushed every available company to the walls, dragging a field cannon through the snow. If Ironpass falls, the northern road lies open.

12 × 12 hex battlefield · North is at the top · Coordinates are column letter and row number

Scenario rules

  • Cards: At the start of each turn, each player draws 4 cards from their activation deck. Alternate playing one card at a time, Empire first on turn 1. Unplayed cards are discarded; shuffle discards back when the deck runs out.
  • Activation: A card names a unit type. It may activate any, all, or none of your living units of that type. Each activated unit may move or attack, not both. Ranged units may shoot instead of moving.
  • Movement: Move through adjacent hexes up to the unit's allowance. Terrain and special movement restrictions are listed below and shown on the map.
  • Combat: Click an enemy in range. The page rolls attack and defence dice automatically; each uncancelled hit removes one figure (one hit point). Ranged attacks need a clear line of sight.
  • Special terrain: The gatehouse F6 is impassable and blocks ranged line of sight. Broken walls grant +1 defence die against ranged attacks. Cannon cannot move; its card permits one ranged attack to range 6. The ogre has 5 hit points and attacks with 4 dice. Cavalry must stop upon entering rocky ground.
  • Starting positions: Empire deploys in columns A–D; Chaos deploys in columns H–L. Coordinates on the map show each unit's fixed starting hex. Each unit occupies one hex; its figures are hit points.
  • Victory: At the end of turn 12, the side with more units adjacent to the gatehouse F6 wins. A side also wins immediately if it destroys every opposing unit. Ties at turn 12 are a draw.

Click one of your units, then an empty highlighted hex to move or a glowing enemy hex to attack. Blue is Empire; red is Chaos. Each marker is one figure and one hit point.
