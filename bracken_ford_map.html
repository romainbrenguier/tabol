Chaos: Orcs H6 and H7; Goblin archers I8; Chaos warriors I5.

Each unit occupies one hex; figures are grouped together in that hex.

Scenario rules

  • Turn cards: At turn start, each player draws 3 cards from their own activation deck. Alternate playing one card at a time, Empire first on turn 1. Discard unplayed cards at turn end; reshuffle discards when a deck runs out.
  • Activation: A card names a unit type. When played, activate any, all, or none of your living units of that type. Each activated unit may move or attack, not both. Ranged units may attack without moving.
  • Movement: A unit may move up to its Move in adjacent hexes. If it follows the road for at least one hex, it may move one additional hex, which must also follow the road. Knights cross the river only at the bridge; infantry may cross at the bridge or ford.
  • Combat: Attacker rolls its Atk dice; defender rolls its Def dice. Skulls score hits; shields cancel hits. Remove one figure per uncancelled hit. Ranged attacks need line of sight; woods block it.
  • Victory: At the end of turn 10, a side wins if it occupies F6 or F7, or destroys every opposing unit. If both occupy the bridge, the side with more figures on F6–F7 wins; tie is a draw. No qualifying winner by turn 10 is a draw.
